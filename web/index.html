    About the project

    Used-car price prediction with machine learning

    A complete project: data cleaning, model comparison, honest evaluation, an API and this website.

    The problem

    Buyers and sellers of used cars in India rarely know what a fair price is. AutoValue predicts the resale price in lakhs of rupees from a car's brand, model, age, kilometres, fuel, gearbox, seller type and ownership. It is a supervised regression problem: the answer is a number.

    The data

    40,440 used-car listings from CarDekho, Cars24 and Spinny, collected in 2025 and 2026 (four public datasets, see DATA_SOURCES.md). Names were matched across sources, and 2,826 duplicate listings were removed, because the same car in both the training and the test data would make the scores look better than they are. That leaves 36,919 unique cars from 38 brands, with their exact variant (VXI, ZXI Plus AMT…), registered up to 2026.

    The method

    1. Clean the listings and split each name into a brand and a model.
    2. Engineer features: age when listed, kilometres (and their logarithm), kilometres per year, the variant's words.
    3. Encode brand, model and exact variant with one-hot and target encoding, and predict the logarithm of the price.
    4. Compare nine algorithms with 5-fold cross-validation, then tune the best three.
    5. Test once on 716 cars the model never saw.
    6. Calibrate a likely price range from the model's past errors.

    Results

    The winner is Gradient Boosting (tuned). On unseen cars its typical error is 15% and its R² is 0.87. Its 80% price range really contained 80% of the true prices.

    Limitations

    • They are asking prices, not final sale prices, and each platform (CarDekho, Cars24, Spinny) prices a little differently.
    • The listings are snapshots from 2025–2026. Prices move over time, so estimates slowly go out of date.
    • Very new cars (2025–2026) and luxury cars have fewer examples, so their estimates are less reliable.
    • There is no information on accidents, condition or servicing.

    Use the API

    Everything on this site comes from a public API. Try it in the interactive docs at /docs, or from the terminal:

    curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8000/predict -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{
  "Car_Name": "Maruti Swift", "Year": 2015, "Kms_Driven": 50000,
  "Fuel_Type": "Petrol", "Seller_Type": "Individual", "Transmission": "Manual", "Owner": 0
}'

    AutoValue: estimates are a guide based on 2025–2026 listings, not a valuation.
